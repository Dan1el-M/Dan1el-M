### 🔎 About me

I'm a Computer Systems student at **Universidad Nacional de Costa Rica (UNA)**, working as a researcher at **LabCIBE**, our cybersecurity lab. My focus is the intersection of **software engineering and industrial cybersecurity (OT/ICS)** — how to identify, secure, and manage assets in operational technology environments.

I'm currently completing my **Graduation Project (TFG)**: a multiprotocol discovery engine for identifying industrial assets over **Modbus TCP** and **OPC UA**, validated in a Factory I/O simulation environment.

- 🔭 Currently building an **OT asset-discovery engine** as my thesis project
- 🌱 Deepening my skills in **penetration testing, Zero Trust Architecture, and industrial protocols**
- 💻 Also working on backend systems, relational databases, and system design
- 📫 Open to internship / junior opportunities in cybersecurity or backend development
